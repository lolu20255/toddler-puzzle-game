Toddler Puzzles · Icon Proposals

Three distinct directions, each toddler-friendly and recognisable at thumbnail size. Pick one to ship, or mix elements. Each downloads as a 1024×1024 JPG ready for App Store Connect.

Chunky red "A" on a pastel-dawn sky. Tells parents "this teaches my child to read" before they tap.

Puzzle Buddy

Four puzzle pieces, each carrying one curriculum cue (A, 1, ball, +), with the smiling face holding the centre. Tells the full story at a glance: puzzles, letters, numbers, shapes, counting.

Icons render at 1024×1024 — App Store Connect's required size. JPG is the format Apple wants for the marketing icon. Drop the file into assets/icon-only.png (rename to .png if you prefer) then npm run cap:assets to regenerate every device-size asset.
