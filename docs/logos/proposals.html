App Icon Proposals

Toddler Puzzles

Three distinct design directions for the App Store icon. Each rendered as inline SVG, exportable as 1024×1024 PNG or JPEG straight from your browser.

Concept 01 · Minimalist

Shape Trio

Circle, triangle, square. The vocabulary every toddler learns first, arranged as a friendly composition. Calm, modern, and instantly recognisable at thumbnail size.

Concept 02 · Gradient Depth

Liquid Rainbow

A dimensional gradient orb on deep night-violet, with a small white smile in the centre. Premium and 2026-modern, but the rainbow palette keeps it firmly in the toddler zone.

Concept 03 · Illustrative

Alphabet Stack

Three stacked toy blocks on a sunny sky-blue background, with the app's mascot sun peeking from the corner. Reads as "preschool learning toy" before the toddler can read.

Pick one, click PNG or JPEG to download at 1024×1024, drop the file into assets/icon-only.png then run npm run cap:assets to generate every device-size asset.
